How to play

Click any two squares to reveal their words. Match a base form with its simple past.

If they don't match, the words stay visible until you press H or Hide & pass. The next player then takes a turn.

Correct pairs go straight into the current player's pile. A correct match earns another turn. New game chooses 24 randomly selected verbs and reshuffles the board.
